Stripe it! — Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Write to support@allikastudio.eu. Mention the device model and iOS version if something goes wrong.

How do I play?

Every number starts a stripe. Drag from the number to draw it; the stripe has to cover exactly as many cells as the number, the number's own cell included. Fill every cell that isn't steel.

What are steel, ice and turn cells?

Steel can't be entered and doesn't need filling. On ice a stripe can't turn: it slides straight through and can't end there. On a turn cell the stripe must turn and can't end there.

How do I open bigger fields?

Solve puzzles on the size before it: 3 on 5×5 open 6×6, 5 on 6×6 open 7×7, then 7, 9, 11 and so on.

How do coins and hints work?

Each solved puzzle earns coins, from 1 on 5×5 to 11 on 15×15. A hint fills in one stripe and costs 3 to 8 coins depending on the field size.

Will I lose a half-finished puzzle?

No. The board is saved after every move, also when you close the app.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The game can use your device language or any of its 11 languages.

The music plays in silent mode. Can I turn it off?

Yes: Settings → Sound → Music.

How do I start over?

Settings → Progress → Reset progress. This clears solved puzzles, best times and coins.
