How to delete your Benzina account and the data linked to it

Delete your account

Two ways to remove your Benzina account and the data linked to it.

Inside the app

Open Benzina, go to Account Settings and choose to delete your account. This is the fastest route and needs nothing from us.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Send a deletion request through this form, or write to support@allikastudio.eu from the email address linked to your account.

What gets deleted

Your Google authentication profile, email address, display name and profile photo URL are permanently purged.

Fuel prices you submitted stay in the database, but completely anonymised — they are no longer linked to you in any way.

More detail

The full description of what the app stores and for how long is in the Benzina privacy policy.
