The Turning Room

The Turning Room is a virtual immersive room, created in After Effects. Real drone footage of the Estonian coast covers the walls and the floor, and a dancing girl stands in the middle. With every turn she makes, the world around her changes: forest, lighthouse, sea, day and night. A symbiosis of classic hand-drawn animation, 3D compositing, real footage and post-production, searching for a balance between technology and artistic image.

The film was created in collaboration with Clip Lab studio, who provided the real drone footage that forms the worlds inside the room. Together we are exploring new images, ideas and forms for a future multimedia project about Estonian landmarks. The Turning Room is one of the experimental variants from this search: an example of how real places can become places that draw attention.
