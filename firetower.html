FireTower: Build & Burn

Build a tower out of sticks and keep it standing while the sticks catch fire. Every stick you add changes the balance, every burnt one stops holding weight — brace it before the tower comes down.

  • Physics-driven tower building — every stick changes the balance
  • Sticks catch fire as you play, and a burnt one stops holding weight
  • Load bars show which sticks are carrying the tower
  • Your best score is kept on the device
  • Settings for language, volume and vibration
  • Works fully offline — the game never connects to the internet

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game stores only your best score and your settings, and only on your device — everything it keeps is listed in the privacy policy. The game is suitable for all ages.
