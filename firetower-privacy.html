Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026
This document covers the FireTower iOS game. See also the FireTower Terms of Use and Support.

What the game stores

FireTower keeps only your best score and your settings (language, volume, vibration, load bars), and only on your device. Like any app data, they may be part of your own device backups.

What it does not collect

No account, name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone, because nothing is collected.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Use “Reset best score” in Settings, or delete the app: that removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Write to support@allikastudio.eu.
