Stripe it! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
This document covers the Stripe it! game. See also the Stripe it! Terms of Use and Support.

What the game collects

Stripe it! does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best times, coins and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads, analytics, accounts or tracking. The game works fully offline and doesn't ask for any permissions.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. You can also reset progress in the game's Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children or anyone else.
