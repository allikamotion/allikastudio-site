Stripe it!

Stripe it! is a logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Every number on the board is the start of a stripe. Draw it out so it covers exactly that many cells, counting the number itself, until every cell is filled. It starts on a small 5×5 field and grows to 15×15, with steel blocks, ice and forced turns along the way.

  • Endless puzzles on eleven field sizes, from 5×5 to 15×15
  • New sizes open as you solve: 3 puzzles on 5×5 open 6×6, then 5, 7, 9 and so on
  • Obstacles that change how you think: steel blocks, ice you slide straight across, and cells where the stripe must turn
  • Every puzzle is generated to have a solution
  • Coins for every solved puzzle. Spend them on hints; bigger fields pay more
  • A half-finished board is saved after every move, even if you close the app
  • Three difficulty levels, a timer you can hide, and best times per puzzle
  • A slow, endless red corridor and a generated ambient soundtrack
  • 11 languages: English, Russian, Estonian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish and Turkish
  • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Progress, coins and settings stay on the device. Suitable for all ages. Everything the game keeps is listed in the privacy policy.
