Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026
This policy covers the allikastudio.eu website. The Benzina mobile app has its own, separate privacy policy.

Who runs this site

allikastudio.eu is operated by Allika Studio OÜ, a company registered in Estonia and based in Tallinn. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@allikastudio.eu.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It has no contact form, no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts, and it sets no cookies of its own.

Nothing you do while browsing this site is recorded by Allika Studio OÜ.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. As with any web host, GitHub's servers automatically record technical details of each request — your IP address, your browser's user-agent string and the address of the page requested — in server logs, which GitHub uses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Allika Studio OÜ does not receive those raw logs.

Fonts

Typefaces on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, operated by Google. Your browser fetches the font files directly from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information as part of that request.

Links to other services

This site links to the App Store, Google Play and GitHub. Once you follow such a link, that service's own privacy policy applies instead of this one.

If you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message and your email address for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of the correspondence. We do not use them for marketing and we do not pass them on to anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. Because this site collects nothing by itself, such a request will normally concern email correspondence. Write to support@allikastudio.eu.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes to this policy

This policy may change over time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
