Benzina: Fuel Prices & Map

Save money on every drive! Benzina is your ultimate companion for finding the cheapest fuel prices and best car washes in the Baltic states (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania) directly on a live, interactive map.

  • Live fuel price comparison (95, 98, Diesel, LPG) across major stations
  • Smart search with an expanding radius algorithm
  • Car wash locator with hours and distances
  • Community-driven price updates with a rewards system
  • Light/dark modes and support for Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Russian and English

No ads and no advertising trackers. Benzina uses analytics and crash reporting only to fix bugs and improve the app — everything it collects is listed in the privacy policy. You can delete your account and all your data directly inside the app at any time.

Download Benzina today, share prices, and start saving on every liter!
