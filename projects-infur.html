INFUR — Flipbook for an In-Store LED Screen

Digital signage · Motion design · Retail

An animated flipbook for the LED screen in the window of INFUR, a fashion store. The video works like a stylist's sketchbook: the pages turn one by one, and each page presents one piece from the collection, a coat, a jacket or a pair of jeans. A short handwritten word and a hand-drawn arrow appear on the page as if written live, pointing at the garment and giving it a character: Fresh, Wild, Soft.

The lines keep a subtle frame-by-frame "boil", so the animation feels personal and handmade rather than corporate. The idea was to present the products in a light, editorial way: no heavy sales graphics, just the garment, one word and a gesture that guides the eye.

On location: the street footage, filmed on a phone, shows the flipbook running in the store window during the day.

My role: concept, art direction, AI image and video generation, motion design, handwritten type animation, technical preparation for the LED display

Tools: After Effects, Photoshop, AI image & video generation
